The story

Decades on the Chippewa.

The SmokeHouse sits on the Chippewa River in Durand, Wisconsin, where it has been slaughtering, dry-aging, and smoking meat for decades. The previous owner ran it for 34 years; the current owners bought the business in 2011 and kept doing things the way the shop had earned its name doing them — by hand, in-house, one order at a time.

A second location in Woodville added a retail counter and deli, so the same product that fills custom-order freezers is there to grab on the way home.

At The SmokeHouse, we know you want local meat, cut right, care that’s worth the trip, not mass-produced. The problem is meat is expensive right now, and too many suppliers are slow to quote, hard to reach, or vague about where “local” actually comes from. We believe real care, craft, and communication shouldn’t be rare — and they shouldn’t be a luxury either. They should be the standard, whether you’re feeding a family or stocking a restaurant kitchen.

Family-run since day one

Feels like your grandparents’ butcher.

No corporate ownership, no private equity behind the counter. Every decision, every order, every conversation happens with the people who built The SmokeHouse.

That’s why the phone gets answered, why your order comes back the way you wrote it, and why the person slicing your lunch meat in Woodville might be the same one who smoked it in Durand.

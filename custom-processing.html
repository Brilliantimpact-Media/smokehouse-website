Start to finish

From your call to your freezer.

  1. Reach out

    Reach out to schedule a slaughter date or place your order, by phone or in person. Call Durand at 715-672-8073. Tell us what you have — or what you want us to find for you — and roughly when you need it.

  2. Get your price

    Get clear pricing upfront, no waiting, no guesswork. You’ll know the hanging-weight rate and the processing charges before your animal is on the schedule, not after.

  3. Pick it up

    Pick up your meat cut exactly to your request, labeled clearly, ready for the freezer. We call you when it’s ready. You shouldn’t have to check in to find out where your order stands.

The cut sheet

You decide, down to the thickness.

The cut sheet is where your order actually gets made. Steak thickness. How many to a package. Roast size. What goes to ground and what goes to sausage. Whether you want the soup bones, the heart, the tongue, the fat for rendering.

If you’ve never filled one out, that’s normal — most people haven’t. Call and we’ll walk you through it, or start with a sample sheet so you can see the choices before you decide.

Split orders

Split orders handled right.

Going in on a beef with your brother, two neighbors, and someone from work? You are not the middleman. We don’t hand you one cut sheet and let you sort out four people’s preferences over text.

Everyone on the delegation list gets their own call and their own cut list. Your sister wants inch-and-a-quarter ribeyes and no stew meat; that’s her conversation with us, not yours. Every share comes back labeled with the right name on it.

You organized the order. That should be the end of your job.

Dry-aged, never rushed

Beef hangs and rests up to 30 days before it ever reaches a cutting board. That time is where tenderness and flavor come from, and it’s the first thing a high-volume plant cuts to move product faster.

Start to finish

From your call to your freezer.

  1. Reach out

    Start with the form at the top of this page and tell us what you have. We will confirm your slaughter date from there, so you are not stuck waiting on a callback.

  2. Claim your cut

    Give us your cutting instructions and we cut it the way you like. This is a family that knows their cuts, and we follow your sheet exactly, so what comes back is what you asked for, not a standard pack someone else decided on.

  3. Boxed and ready for the freezer

    Your order comes back boxed, labeled and ready to go straight in the freezer. We call you when it is ready, so you are not guessing.

The cut sheet

You decide, down to the thickness.

The cut sheet is where your order actually gets made. Steak thickness. How many to a package. Roast size. What goes to ground and what goes to sausage. Whether you want the soup bones, the heart, the tongue, the fat for rendering.

If you’ve never filled one out, that’s normal, most people haven’t. Call and we’ll walk you through it, or start with a sample sheet so you can see the choices before you decide.

Split orders

Split orders handled right.

Going in on a beef with your brother, two neighbors, and someone from work? You are not the middleman. We don’t hand you one cut sheet and let you sort out four people’s preferences over text.

Everyone on the delegation list gets their own call and their own cut list. Your sister wants a quarter or a half, that’s her conversation with us, not yours.

You organized the order. That should be the end of your job.

Dry-aged, never rushed

Beef hangs for a minimum of 7 days before it ever reaches a cutting board. That time is where tenderness and flavor come from, and it’s the first thing a high-volume plant cuts to move product faster.

Don’t have an animal? We’ll source one.

You don’t need livestock to fill a freezer with local meat. Tell us the size of order you’re after and we’ll find the animal from one of our local farmers.

Labeled so you can read it in six months

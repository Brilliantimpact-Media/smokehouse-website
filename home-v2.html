Why we’re here

We know how frustrating it is to not trust what meat is in your freezer or your grocery cart. You deserve food that’s local and cut by a team who cares.

The SmokeHouse has been earning this craft for decades. We source, process, and smoke meat the way it’s supposed to be done, not the way a plant does it. Every order gets the same care, whether it’s a split beef order or a standing wholesale account.

Why we’re here

We know how frustrating it is to not trust what meat is in your freezer or your grocery cart. You deserve food that’s local and cut by a team who cares.

The SmokeHouse has been earning this craft for decades. We source, process, and smoke meat the way it’s supposed to be done, not the way a plant does it.

01

You have an animal, or want one sourced

Bring us yours, or let us find it. You pick the date, fill out a cut sheet, and pick up exactly what you asked for, labeled clearly, ready for the freezer. Splitting with family or neighbors? Everyone on the list gets their own call and their own cut list.

Real
dry-aging

Beef hangs and rests a minimum of 7 days before it ever reaches a cutting board. That time builds flavor and tenderness most competitors skip entirely, because it costs time and space they don’t have.

Custom-
to-order

Every order is cut to your exact specs, not pulled from a pre-set package or a standard cut list. What you order is what you get, not what’s convenient to produce.

Over a decade of craft,
one crew

Dry-aging, hand-cutting, and smoking every order ourselves, right here in the Chippewa and St. Croix Valley. Not a plant three states away.

Family-run
since day one

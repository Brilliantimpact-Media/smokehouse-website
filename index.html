Why we do it this way

We know how frustrating it is to not trust what meat is in your freezer or your grocery cart. You deserve food that’s local and cut by a team who cares.

The SmokeHouse has been earning this craft for decades. We source, process, and smoke meat the way it’s supposed to be done, not the way a plant does it. Every order gets the same care, whether it’s a split beef order or a standing wholesale account.

Meat isn’t a commodity. It’s a craft — and real care, clear communication, and honest cutting shouldn’t be rare or expensive. They should just be the standard.

What you don’t get somewhere else

Four things worth the drive.

Real dry-aging

Beef hangs and rests up to 30 days before it ever reaches a cutting board. That time builds flavor and tenderness most competitors skip entirely because it costs time and space they don’t have.

Custom-to-order

Every order is cut to your exact specs, not pulled from a pre-set package or a standard cut list. What you order is what you get, not what’s convenient to produce.

Decades of craft, one crew

Dry-aging, hand-cutting, and smoking every order ourselves, right here in the Chippewa and St. Croix Valley. Not a plant three states away.

Family-run since day one

No corporate ownership, no private equity behind the counter. Every decision, every order, every conversation happens with the people who built The SmokeHouse.

Real
dry-aging

Beef hangs and rests up to 30 days before it ever reaches a cutting board. That time builds flavor and tenderness most competitors skip entirely, because it costs time and space they don’t have.

Custom-
to-order

Every order is cut to your exact specs, not pulled from a pre-set package or a standard cut list. What you order is what you get, not what’s convenient to produce.

one crew

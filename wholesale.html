What an account looks like

Built for repeat orders.

Pricing that is fast and clear, doesn’t waste your time

Ask for a quote and get one while it’s still useful. If you run a standing order, you’ll know about price changes before they hit your invoice.

The same product, every order

The case at a big distributor changes with whoever’s cheapest that month. Ours doesn’t. Same crew cutting, same spec, order after order.

Volume, honestly discussed

We’re a small crew, and we’d rather tell you exactly what we can commit to weekly than overpromise and short your kitchen. If your volume fits, it fits for good.

A local story your customers can taste

“Our meat comes from a butcher twenty minutes away” means something on a menu, a shelf tag, or a school lunch tray. We’ll back it up, come tour the shop and see how it’s cut.

For schools & restaurants

Local isn’t a label here. It’s the address.

Districts and kitchens are being asked where their food comes from. With us the answer is specific: sourced, processed, and smoked in Durand, Wisconsin, by a family-owned shop, not relabeled somewhere along a distributor’s chain.

Against a distributor, that’s the tiebreaker: identical carton price, but only one option gives your customers a name and a town.
